If you've transferred money to another Australian recently, you've probably used PayID — even if you didn't realise it. Instead of entering a BSB and account number, you just type in someone's phone number or email address.

PayID is part of Australia's New Payments Platform (NPP), which enables near-instant transfers between Australian bank accounts 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Here's how it works for bill splitting.

## What Is PayID?

PayID is a unique identifier — usually a mobile number or email address — that's linked to your bank account. When someone sends you a PayID transfer, it arrives in seconds, not business days.

Every major Australian bank supports PayID:
- CommBank
- ANZ
- Westpac
- NAB
- ING
- Macquarie
- Bendigo Bank
- Bank Australia
- St.George, Bank of Melbourne, BankSA (Westpac subsidiaries)
- And essentially every other bank on the NPP network

## How to Set Up Your PayID

1. Log in to your banking app
2. Go to Settings or PayID settings
3. Register a PayID — usually your mobile number
4. Done

Once it's set up, anyone with your phone number can send you money instantly.

## The Problem PayID Solves for Bill Splitting

The old way of settling up after a group trip was painful:
1. Ask everyone for their BSB and account number
2. Type it all into your banking app (and hope you don't make a typo)
3. Send the transfer
4. Wait for confirmation it arrived

With PayID:
1. Ask everyone for their phone number (you probably already have it)
2. Open your banking app, type in their number
3. Amount is transferred instantly

Still better than the old way, but it requires switching between apps and manually entering the amount.

## How EasySplit Makes PayID Even Easier

EasySplit integrates with PayID at the app level. Here's how it works:

**Store PayIDs once.** Each person in your trip saves their PayID in their EasySplit profile. You only do this once, ever.

**Tap to settle.** When it's time to settle up, EasySplit shows you exactly what you owe each person. Tap Pay Now and your banking app opens with:
- The recipient's PayID already entered
- The exact settlement amount pre-filled

You just confirm and send. No switching between apps to check the amount. No risk of entering the wrong figure.

**Mark as settled.** After transferring, tap Mark as Settled in EasySplit. The balance updates immediately.

## How Much Does PayID Cost?

PayID transfers themselves are free — it's part of the NPP infrastructure that all Australian banks have built into their systems.

EasySplit's core expense tracking is also free. When EasySplit Pay launches (a future feature that processes the transfer within the app), there will be a small transaction fee. But the current PayID integration — which just pre-fills your banking app — is completely free.

## A Note on Security

PayID transfers are just bank transfers. The money moves between your real bank accounts. EasySplit never sees your banking credentials, never touches the money, and never stores your account details — only your PayID identifier (usually your phone number).
