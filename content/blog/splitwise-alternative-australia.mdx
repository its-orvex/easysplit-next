If you've been using Splitwise to track expenses with your friends or housemates, you've probably noticed the new limitations on the free plan. In 2023, Splitwise introduced a cap of 3 expense entries per day for free users, along with mandatory ads.

Three expenses a day sounds reasonable until you're on a weekend trip to Bali and ordering breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a round of cocktails. Suddenly you're locked out of logging expenses and either someone's paying out of pocket or you're trying to remember it all for tomorrow.

Here are the five best alternatives for Australians — all free, and all with better limits than the current Splitwise free tier.

## 1. EasySplit (Best for Australians)

EasySplit is a new Australian bill-splitting app built specifically for the way Australians manage money. It's completely free with no daily limits, no ads in the app, and no paywalled features.

**What makes it different:**
- PayID settlement — store everyone's PayID once, and settling up is one tap
- Receipt scanning — point your camera at any receipt and the app fills in the details
- Guest links — share a link so friends can see balances without downloading anything
- Built for AUD with Australian bank support

EasySplit is a PWA, which means you can install it from your browser without the App Store. Works on iPhone and Android.

## 2. Tricount

Tricount is a solid free alternative that's been around for a while. It has unlimited expense logging, which is its biggest advantage over the current Splitwise free tier.

The downsides: it shows ads, doesn't support receipt scanning, and has no PayID integration. Settlement requires you to manually transfer money and mark it as done.

Good for: Simple equal splits in groups where everyone is happy to do manual bank transfers.

## 3. Settle Up

Settle Up is a cross-platform expense tracker with unlimited expenses on the free tier. The interface is a bit dated but it works reliably.

It supports multiple currencies and has a decent settlement calculator. No receipt scanning, no PayID.

Good for: International trips where you need multi-currency support.

## 4. Splitwise Pro

If you want to stay with Splitwise, the Pro plan at $7.99/month removes the daily limits and ads, and adds receipt scanning and other features.

Whether it's worth $8/month depends on how often you use it. If you're tracking a house with ongoing expenses, probably yes. For occasional group trips, probably not.

## 5. A Shared Google Sheet

Underrated option for simple situations. A shared spreadsheet with running totals works surprisingly well for house expenses where everyone trusts each other and is comfortable with basic maths.

Downsides: no notifications, no settlement calculator, lots of manual work.

## The Bottom Line

For Australians, EasySplit is the strongest free alternative — it's the only one with PayID integration and it's specifically built for the Australian market. For everyone else, Tricount is the most capable free option that doesn't restrict your expense logging.
